name: Homepage visual direction
description: The user's desired homepage background and section styling for Fresh Stock Xchange.
---

Use the wood-and-compass palette (#C8651B, #E8883A, #F0A458, #8B3A0F, #7B3A14), the latest supplied natural brown wood texture behind homepage content, and pure white product card surfaces. The header and footer use the same dark brown wood texture and overlay; header text, borders, and icons are white, with the logo inside a white circle. Keep section wrappers transparent with no panel backgrounds or borders; retain individual product cards with square, edge-to-edge image edges, bottom breathing room for content, and section titles aligned with their grids. The footer brand block reuses the header logo inside its white circle beside a bold white “Fresh Stock Xchange” wordmark and prominent white copy. Keep it in the first column, left of Customer Care; retain Customer Care, contact, and map content, omit the Explore list, and keep these four groups in one desktop row. Footer text, icons, outlines, and dividers are pure white and slightly bold; omit separate Call Us, Shop Fresh Catch, and WhatsApp buttons, but keep the phone number in contact details. Slightly enlarge Customer Care and Get in Touch text and contact icons.

**Why:** the user requested this palette, most recently replaced the homepage wood background with a natural brown wood texture, rejected tinted section backgrounds and nested cards, and specified that the header should use the footer's dark wood with white content and a white logo circle. They also asked for a larger header on desktop and mobile, plus a bold white footer wordmark, a larger logo inside its circle, pure-white footer text/icons/lines, no footer buttons, and slightly larger Customer Care/Get in Touch content.

**How to apply:** use the latest supplied warm golden wood texture as the home page background without a color overlay; keep product card surfaces pure white and align content gutters, section titles, and card images consistently. The header and footer should share the dark footer wood treatment; make all header text/icons white, wrap the header logo in a white circle, and keep the header taller with larger text and icons on desktop and mobile while preventing narrow screens from overflowing. In the footer, place the logo/wordmark/tagline/copy at the left of Customer Care, omit the Explore group and separate Call Us, Shop Fresh Catch, and WhatsApp buttons, keep the phone number in Contact, use pure white semibold-or-bolder text/icons/borders, and keep the brand, Customer Care, Contact, and Find Us side by side on desktop with responsive stacking on smaller screens.
