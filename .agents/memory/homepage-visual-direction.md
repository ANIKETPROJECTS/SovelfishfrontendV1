name: Homepage visual direction
description: The user's desired homepage background and section styling for Fresh Stock Xchange.
---

Use the wood-and-compass palette (#C8651B, #E8883A, #F0A458, #8B3A0F, #7B3A14), the same brown-wood texture as the footer behind homepage content without a color overlay, and white-painted wood texture on the header and card surfaces. Keep section wrappers transparent with no panel backgrounds or borders; retain individual product cards with square image edges and avoid decorative bubbles. The footer uses a dark overlay on the shared brown-wood texture for text contrast.

**Why:** the user requested this palette, asked to reuse the footer's wood texture on the homepage, and chose a separate white-painted wood texture for headers and cards after rejecting tinted backgrounds, nested cards, and decorative bubbles.

**How to apply:** use the footer's brown-wood image for the home page background without a color overlay, and use the white-painted wood texture for the header and card surfaces; leave section wrappers transparent, retain individual product cards with square image edges, and preserve existing layout and behavior.
