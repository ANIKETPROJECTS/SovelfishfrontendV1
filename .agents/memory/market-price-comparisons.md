---
name: Market price comparisons
description: Product requirements for the storefront's market-style price board.
---

The production market board must show actual catalog prices and only report movements created by admin-saved price changes. Compare the first price update in an India-local day with the prior close; compare later updates that day with the first update's price. Daily low/high come only from the prior close and recorded prices for that day. Sample weights, prices, ranges, and trends are allowed in the Replit development preview only and must never be persisted or shown in production.

**Why:** The client wants a restaurant-style market concept that communicates real price rises and falls without fabricated production data, while allowing a filled visual preview.

**How to apply:** Keep the comparison rule consistent in storefront displays, admin price workflows, and any future price-history reporting. Show an honest neutral state when no price history exists.
