---
name: Market price comparisons
description: Product requirements for the storefront's market-style price board.
---

The market board must show actual catalog prices and only report movements created by admin-saved price changes. Compare the first price update in an India-local day with the prior close; compare later updates that day with the first update's price. Daily low/high come only from the prior close and recorded prices for that day; do not invent historical values.

**Why:** The client wants a restaurant-style market concept that communicates real price rises and falls without fabricated sample movement.

**How to apply:** Keep the comparison rule consistent in storefront displays, admin price workflows, and any future price-history reporting. Show an honest neutral state when no price history exists.
